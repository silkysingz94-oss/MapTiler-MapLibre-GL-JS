METHODOLOGY & DATA NOTES

GPS methodology

Device coordinates are obtained through the browser Geolocation API. Accuracy is reported by the device and may vary with satellite visibility, hardware, network conditions and obstructions.

Travel-time methodology

Driving routes use the public OSRM road-routing service. Walking and cycling are indicative estimates based on straight-line distance and assumed speeds, not pedestrian or cycling network routes. Actual travel time depends on road conditions, traffic and route choice.

Data limitations

Town coordinates are illustrative locations, not official administrative boundary centroids. This dashboard does not replace authoritative GBoS boundaries or verified road-network data. MapTiler services require a valid key and are subject to account limits and terms.
